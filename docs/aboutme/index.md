#About Me
Hello, my name is Nicholas, and I am a junior at UNC Charlotte pursuing a bachelor’s degree in mechanical engineering. I've always been curious about how things function, how to solve problems, and how to create and construct things. I've been able to combine these interests while working on my math, physics, CAD, and engineering analytical skills thanks to mechanical engineering. Motorsports and automotive engineering particularly intrigue me, and I want to work in a field where I can use my engineering expertise to design and develop automobiles and mechanical systems.

One of the things that drew me to engineering is the ability to take an idea and turn it into something functional. I've discovered that engineering is much more than just designing things through my academics and side projects. Performance, efficiency, dependability, safety, cost, and the project's realistic constraints are all factors that an engineer must take into account. I like the process of creating something, testing it, finding issues, and refining it until the desired outcome is achieved. Throughout my time at UNC Charlotte, I want to keep improving these abilities and gaining experience that will help me get ready for a career in engineering.

Defending an engineering decision, in my opinion, is being able to justify the selection of a specific design or solution. An engineer should be able to prove that a choice is safe, dependable, and suitable for its intended use using calculations, testing, research, and engineering principles. I feel comfortable defending basic core engineering choices at this stage of my schooling, including those dealing with statics, material selection, and straightforward design procedures. I am aware, though, that I still have a lot to learn before I can successfully justify difficult engineering choices. I want to become an engineer who can make well-supported decisions and stand behind the work I generate as I acquire more knowledge and experience.
